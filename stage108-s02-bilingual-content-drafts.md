This stage prepares two bilingual content candidates from the Stage 108 site-gap review. The candidates are review material only: Muse must review the facts and wording, and the current Astro source must be re-read before any production change.

## Candidate A: contact path

Question: How should a trade business start a conversation before commissioning a custom AI workflow?

The proposed answer starts with one observable workflow, its inputs, output recipient, trigger and human approval points. A redacted one-page brief can then be used to discuss scope, existing systems, access and acceptance through the existing WeChat or WhatsApp paths. The contact entry starts a conversation; it is not a quote, project approval or outcome guarantee.

Proposed CTA pair: `用一个流程开始沟通` / `Start with one workflow`.

## Candidate B: mobile experience

Question: How can a mobile visitor read an AI services page and complete the next step?

The proposed UX acceptance text asks reviewers to check 360x800 and 390x844 views, readable content, no forced horizontal scrolling, tappable controls, reachable contact actions, and a chat panel that does not cover the primary content. It should remain an acceptance aid for existing service/resource pages rather than a thin SEO page.

Proposed CTA pair: `在手机上开始沟通` / `Start a conversation on mobile`.
