# Stage 53: Muse Website Operations Readiness

Date: 2026-10-18 (Asia/Shanghai)

This stage defines a daily operating protocol for Muse to maintain the website's own Chinese and English Insights content. The scope is limited to paired Markdown articles in the two Insights locales. No Zhihu or WeChat Official Account sections are part of the site.

The protocol covers topic intake, bilingual drafting, fact and case-permission checks, preview/build validation, release receipts, rollback, daily status reporting, and weekly review. Content quality governs publication frequency; a daily queue check does not require publishing a low-value article every day. Unknown, unmeasured, inaccessible, and unexecuted states remain distinct from zero or success.

Muse's actual source access, independent builds, recurring task execution, isolated production permissions, publishing, and rollback have not yet been verified. The existing GEO goal is not proof of a scheduled website operation. Strict HTTPS validation for the production domain failed because its certificate is expired. Until the production prerequisites and Muse's own operating path are verified, Muse may prepare bilingual drafts and local previews but must not publish to production or change server configuration.

This public summary contains no credentials, customer data, private conversation, server account, or production configuration. It does not claim deployment, AI citations, leads, or sales results.
