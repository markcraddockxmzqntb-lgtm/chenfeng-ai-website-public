# Inquiry workflow brief

Blank template · v1.0 · 2026-10-20 · [Guide](stage105-inquiry-kit-guide.md)

Start with one process. Write “to confirm” where information is missing. You do not need to provide original customer records, passwords or keys. Keep completed copies locally; do not upload them to this public repository.

| ID | Question | Your situation |
|---|---|---|
| B01 | What should improve first: classification, extraction, translation, reply drafts or salesperson handoff? | To complete |
| B02 | Who handles it today, using which inbox, spreadsheet or business system? Roughly how many inquiries per day and in which languages? | To complete |
| B03 | What triggers the workflow, and what must not trigger it? | To complete |
| B04 | Which input materials are permitted? What are their versions, owners and sources? | To complete |
| B05 | Which output fields, format and recipient are needed? How will the source be retained for review? | To complete |
| B06 | Who approves each action: price, delivery date, commitments, sending and system updates? | To complete |
| B07 | Which accounts, APIs and permissions are available? Which are read-only or writable? Which eligibility requirements or costs still need confirmation? | To complete |
| B08 | How should missing data, conflicts, duplicates or API failures be flagged and handed to a person? | To complete |
| B09 | Which approved samples will be used for acceptance? Define expectations for fields, sources, missing data, approval and failure paths. | To complete |
| B10 | What is explicitly outside the first phase? | To complete |
| B11 | Who maintains materials, handles exceptions and approves updates? How will provider usage costs and maintenance be agreed? | To complete |
| B12 | What is still missing, and who will confirm it? You may separately provide a business contact if you wish. | To complete |

## A short message for an initial discussion

We want to improve [B01] first. Today [B02] handles the process. It should use [B04] and produce [B05]. Approval by [B06] is required, and [B10] is outside the first phase. We still need to confirm [B07/B12] and would like to discuss scope and acceptance criteria.

## Before sharing

- [ ] Separate fictional examples from your actual situation; leave unknown conditions unconfirmed.
- [ ] Remove personal information, customer lists, sensitive commercial information and credentials.
- [ ] State human approval points, exception handling and the first-phase boundary.
- [ ] Share only with the party you want to consult, rather than this public repository.
