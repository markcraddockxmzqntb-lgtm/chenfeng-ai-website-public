# Stage 94 — Real consumer-platform baseline preserved

Seven consumer AI platforms were checked in the browser. Eight access checks include a later successful Doubao check after the owner logged in. Four complete answers were captured across ChatGPT, Grok and Doubao: three brand prompts and one non-brand inquiry-workflow prompt. These are operator-issued probes, not customers' private questions, demand volume or a representative platform benchmark.

The baseline revealed brand confusion, mixed sources, an outdated operating entity and unsupported service claims. Four immutable evidence bundles were validated, imported into the private production dashboard, read back and backed up. Historical answers remain tied to the website version visible at sampling time. The subsequent operating-entity correction is recorded separately in stage 95.

Login and region-access blocks are access observations, not indexing failures. Sampling region is unknown and the device was a desktop browser. Grok source capture is partial. Official citation and platform-wide indexing rates remain unknown under the evidence contract. Raw answers, source captures and screenshots remain private. No synthetic probe is counted as a qualified inquiry or acquired customer.
