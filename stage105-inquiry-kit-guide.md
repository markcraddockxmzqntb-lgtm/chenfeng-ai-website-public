## 从这里开始

1. [下载完整 ZIP](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/markcraddockxmzqntb-lgtm/chenfeng-ai-website-public/main/stage105-inquiry-workflow-kit.zip)，解压后在本地使用；或直接打开[中文简报](stage105-inquiry-brief-zh.md)、[English brief](stage105-inquiry-brief-en.md)。
2. 只选一个希望改善的流程，填写已知情况；没有答案的项目保留“待确认”。
3. 用[空白交接表](stage105-inquiry-handoff-template.csv)约定输出字段；参照[示例交接表](stage105-inquiry-handoff-examples.csv)和[双语输入输出示例](stage105-inquiry-workflow-examples.json)讨论边界。
4. 希望进一步评估时，自愿分享脱敏后的简报，再讨论接口、首期范围和验收方式。

CSV 使用带 BOM 的 UTF-8 编码，可用常见表格软件打开；若全部内容落在同一列，请用“从文本/CSV导入”并选择逗号分隔。Markdown 可用文本编辑器打开；JSON 供技术同事阅读。所有示例均为虚构演示，不是客户记录或已交付系统。

填写后的副本留在你自己的设备。不要把客户名单、原始询盘、商业文件、密码或密钥提交到公开仓库。只分享你愿意且有权分享的脱敏资料。

## Turn scattered inquiries into a workflow your team can hand over

For foreign-trade and cross-border e-commerce teams in mainland China and Hong Kong. Start with one process: where inquiries arrive, what information is available, what the output should contain, and who must approve it.

This free kit contains templates and fictional examples. No account or model call is required. It does not connect to your inbox, send messages or update a CRM.

1. [Download the ZIP](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/markcraddockxmzqntb-lgtm/chenfeng-ai-website-public/main/stage105-inquiry-workflow-kit.zip) and extract it, or open the [English brief](stage105-inquiry-brief-en.md).
2. Complete what you know. Leave unanswered items as “to confirm”.
3. Agree the handoff fields using the [blank CSV](stage105-inquiry-handoff-template.csv). Compare the [example CSV](stage105-inquiry-handoff-examples.csv) and [bilingual JSON examples](stage105-inquiry-workflow-examples.json).
4. Share a redacted brief voluntarily if you want to discuss integration, a first pilot and acceptance criteria.

CSV files use UTF-8 with a BOM. If your spreadsheet opens a single column, import as a comma-delimited CSV. Markdown opens in a text editor; JSON is provided for technical colleagues. Keep completed copies on your own device. Do not upload customer records, original inquiries, commercial documents, passwords or keys to this public repository.
