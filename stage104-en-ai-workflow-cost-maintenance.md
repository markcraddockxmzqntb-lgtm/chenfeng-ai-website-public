Custom AI workflow cost depends on the actions in scope, material quality, APIs and access, acceptance requirements and maintenance. Define the inputs, outputs, approvals and failure handling for one workflow. Then separate delivery costs from ongoing operation to make proposals comparable.

## Agree on four things before a quote

1. Who starts the workflow and when, such as a salesperson selecting an authorized inquiry.
2. Where inputs come from and who reviews and updates them.
3. What the workflow delivers, such as source-linked fields and a reply draft for review.
4. Who approves prices, delivery commitments, system updates and external sending, and who handles failures.

Start with the [one-page workflow brief](/en/resources/workflow-brief/). Mark uncertain APIs or account conditions as “to confirm.” Original customer files, passwords and keys are unnecessary for an initial assessment.

## What changes scope and cost

| Factor | Practical effect on the proposal |
|---|---|
| Material quality and versions | Preparation, deduplication, source references and future updates |
| Systems and access | Available APIs, read/write scope, test access and platform eligibility |
| Tasks and approvals | Drafting only, or approval, updates, notifications and human handoff |
| Reliability requirements | Exceptions, deduplication, retries, logs, backups and recovery |
| Hosting and maintenance | Runtime location, access controls, support coverage and ownership |

The same page count or model name does not imply the same work. This article provides no fixed price or delivery schedule; both depend on an agreed scope.

## Define the deliverables

The proposal or contract should specify a usable customer entry point, authorized integrations, hosting, outputs, acceptance records, operating instructions and maintenance handoff. Agree separately on source code, licensing, third-party accounts and usage rights. A prompt, prototype or API implementation alone does not establish a complete operational service.

Use authorized samples to check normal inputs, missing information, duplicates, expired access and service failures. Results should be traceable to inputs. Agree on the test scope, measures and acceptable differences before development, then review the records. Use the [AI readiness checklist](/en/resources/ai-readiness/) to check materials, access and responsibilities first.

## Separate delivery costs from recurring charges

Itemize development, configuration, material preparation, integration testing and delivery as appropriate. Ongoing charges may include models, hosting, platform subscriptions, messages, storage and maintenance. Clarify which suppliers the customer pays directly and what a maintenance arrangement covers.

Charging follows each selected provider's current rules. If usage alerts or spending controls are required, confirm the available measurement, behavior at the limit and human fallback. An estimate is not a billing guarantee.

## Assign maintenance responsibilities

Name the owners for source updates, exception review, API changes, backup recovery and access revocation. New channels, features or business rules may change scope. Response times, support windows and additional charges should follow the agreement.

The [trade workspace demo](/en/cases/trade-workspace/) can support a discussion about the user journey. It provides no verified savings, revenue or live integration results. Assess a project through agreed conditions, real integration checks and acceptance evidence.

If one repeated task takes time, describe the current process, desired output and approver. Use the page's WeChat or WhatsApp options to discuss a first scope that can be tested.
